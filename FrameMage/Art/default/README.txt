Put replacement UI art here as <TextureName>.png.

Get the texture names: in game press Ctrl+F9, then open ../../dumps/textures.txt.
Match the original's size and aspect ratio (the dump shows WxH); PNG with alpha is supported.
Press Ctrl+F10 in game to reload after editing files.

Make another skin by copying this folder (e.g. Art/dark/) and setting skin = "dark" in config/settings.lua.
Only ship art you made yourself or have permission to use (Jagex Community Modding Guidelines).
