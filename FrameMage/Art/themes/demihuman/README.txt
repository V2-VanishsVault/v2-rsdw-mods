Demi-human theme (the default): moonstone and wispy gold light, High Elf meets Dwarven.

  <TextureName>.png      replaces that game texture everywhere it's used
  materials.lua          re-colors animated effects (start from materials.example.lua)
  ui/                    Frame Mage's own panels, title bars and keycaps (made by tools/make_theme.py)
  theme.lua              colors and sizes for Frame Mage's own panels

Texture names and sizes: in game press Ctrl+F9, then open ../../../dumps/textures.txt. Match the size and
aspect ratio shown there; PNG with alpha is supported. Ctrl+F10 reloads after editing.

The other themes (warrior, ranger, wizard) fall back to this folder for anything they don't have.
Only ship art you made yourself or have permission to use (Jagex Community Modding Guidelines).
